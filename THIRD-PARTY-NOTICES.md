## Text recognition models

The embedded text detection and recognition models are PP-OCR models from PaddleOCR, converted to ONNX.

- PP-OCRv4 text detection and PP-OCRv5 Latin text recognition, from [monkt/paddleocr-onnx](https://huggingface.co/monkt/paddleocr-onnx) and [RapidAI/RapidOCR](https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR). Original models: [PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR).
